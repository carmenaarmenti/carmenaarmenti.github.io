About me

"Have the courage to follow your heart and intuition."

I'm a hard worker and enthusiastic learner driven by curiosity and a desire to make a difference. I believe in pushing beyond my comfort zone, working collaboratively, and exchanging knowledge with others. I'm results-focused and organized, with a strong work ethic balanced by humility and realism

My philosophy? Care deeply about things without worrying excessively about outcomes. Work hard, but trust the process.

Skills and Interests

I have a natural curiosity that drives me to question "why" and "how." This means I don't settle for easy answers—I search for deeper understanding and try to stay open-minded. This curiosity drew me to software engineering, where I'm particularly fascinated by software sonification, program comprehension, software evolution, and visualization.

Through my BSc and MSc, I've learned languages like C, Java, Python, and JavaScript, along with tools like Git and GitHub. I've also developed soft skills in communication, problem-solving, and adaptability—but I'm always learning.

Outside of tech, music recharges me. So does nature, reading, and time with family and friends. I enjoy staying active, cooking for the people I love, and exploring ideas in philosophy and neuroscience. I value tradition but embrace progress.
